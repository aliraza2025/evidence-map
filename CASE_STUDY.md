# Nukta — product case study

Stage: independent prototype.

## Product contribution

An evidence-first discovery workbench with exact quote validation, editable hypotheses, explicit RICE assumptions, and local LlamaIndex/MiniLM retrieval.

Original domain framing, workflow and interface design, implementation with AI assistance, input validation, tests, and source-linked documentation. Foundation: https://github.com/run-llama/llama_index.

## Verification

Local LlamaIndex embedding retrieval. The repository includes the relevant fixtures, tests, and retained reports.

## Tradeoffs and limits

Exact quote matching does not establish logical support for a hypothesis. Keyword grouping and model-backed retrieval are different modes. The sample interviews are fictional, and RICE values start as operator assumptions.

## Next product test

Evaluate retrieval recall and interpretation quality separately on permission-cleared interviews.
