Nukta

Evidence-led product discovery

An independent AI product experiment by Ali Raza.

The example interviews are fictional. Exact quote matching verifies the citation text, not the quality of an interpretation. RICE inputs start as editable assumptions, not measured outcomes.

01 / Start with what was said

One observation per line: Participant label | exact interview text. Use labels rather than names.

Keyword mode runs in your browser. Local AI sends the transcript to your configured Ollama server when you click Map.

02 / Decide what deserves a test

Select an opportunity. Follow its evidence. Adjust your assumptions.

03 / Take the reasoning with you

Export the sources, hypotheses, review status, and assumptions together.

Source notebook

04 / Search the evidence locally

The local integration uses LlamaIndex with a MiniLM embedding model. It returns source text, not a generated answer.
